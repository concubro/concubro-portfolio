Rope Game

Rope Game began with Sterling, a good concept, and some napkin sketches. That seemed like enough reason to spend a frankly unreasonable amount of time making a cooperative first-person game about operating a rope in an ancient off-world tower.

One to six people haul strange creatures in through a dimensional portal and discover how quickly teamwork becomes workplace misconduct. It is still in pre-alpha, but local and remote multiplayer work, four creatures have made it through the machinery, and EX Siting remains a perfectly responsible company to work for.
