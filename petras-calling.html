Petra’s Calling

Petra’s Calling is a game idea I’m too personally invested in to explain properly yet. It is a small first-person narrative comedy about work, responsibility, and the administrative machinery beneath heroic stories.

The part that interests me is rarely the canonical hero. It is the person at a desk whose quiet approvals keep the whole glorious apparatus moving, and what happens when that person has to come to grips with a moral neutrality that may never have existed.

That is enough for now. It is early, private, unofficial, and adjacent to a universe people may recognize from the name. I am keeping the rest close until Petra calls.
